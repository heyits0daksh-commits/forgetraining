Before You Start

TRAIN SMART.

Read this first. FORGE prescribes training loads based on the numbers you enter — it is not a medical device and does not replace a coach, physiotherapist, or doctor. Consult a healthcare professional before starting any new training program, especially if you are pregnant, recovering from injury, or managing a health condition. Stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, or anything unusual, and always prioritize proper form over the numbers on screen. You are responsible for training within your own limits.
