Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: August 21, 2026
FORGE is not a medical device, and it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Nothing in this app should be treated as a substitute for professional medical judgment.

What FORGE Is

FORGE generates workout and training program suggestions using an automated, rule-based engine. It considers factors you provide — such as reported injuries, training goals, and experience level — to filter and prioritize exercises. This is a prescription-style output based on general fitness heuristics, not an individualized clinical assessment.

What FORGE Is Not

  • It is not a diagnosis of any injury, condition, or disease.
  • It is not a substitute for evaluation by a doctor, physical therapist, athletic trainer, or other licensed healthcare provider.
  • It cannot account for your full medical history, imaging results, or a hands-on physical examination.
  • Its injury-related filtering is a best-effort computational model and may be incomplete, outdated, or wrong for your specific situation.

Before You Start

If you have a current injury, a chronic condition, are recovering from surgery, are pregnant, or have any concern about whether exercise is safe for you, consult a qualified healthcare professional before following any program generated by FORGE.

Stop Immediately If

  • You feel sharp, shooting, or worsening pain during an exercise.
  • You experience dizziness, chest pain, shortness of breath, or any other concerning symptom.
  • An exercise otherwise feels wrong for your body, regardless of what the app suggests.

Your own judgment and, where needed, a healthcare professional's guidance always take priority over any suggestion from this app.

Assumption of Risk

Exercise carries an inherent risk of injury. By using FORGE, you acknowledge that you are voluntarily choosing to follow its suggestions at your own risk, and that FORGE's creators are not liable for injuries, losses, or damages arising from your use of the app.

Emergencies

FORGE is not equipped to handle medical emergencies. If you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.
